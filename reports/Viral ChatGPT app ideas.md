# Ship shareable verdicts, monetize outside ChatGPT

As of October 2026, an indie developer in India has a real shot at a viral ChatGPT app if it does three things. It should produce a **personal, shareable verdict card** ("you and your partner score 27/36", "your thali has 18 g protein", "your resume gets a 4/10 roast"). It should be **India-specific enough that the big global brands are not there**. And it should be built as an **MCP-based app inside a ChatGPT "plugin"**, not as a custom GPT. Custom GPTs are being retired: creation reportedly closed on 25 September and the scheduled shutdown is 11 December 2026. The audience is very large: OpenAI cited **1.2 billion weekly users** at DevDay on 29 September, and **India is the #2 market with about 100 million weekly users**. The weak points are distribution and money. No third-party app has had a documented viral breakout. Bloomberg reported in March that results "lag". OpenAI's rules still **ban selling subscriptions, credits or any digital goods inside ChatGPT**, including indirect freemium upsells, and they ban ads inside apps. So every idea below treats ChatGPT as a free front door and earns money somewhere else: physical goods sold through external checkout, a paid web product the user signs into, or B2B deals. My ranked shortlist is a two-person Kundli/Guna-Milan compatibility card, a JEE/NEET/UPSC previous-year-question practice arena, a resume roast-and-redline tool, an Indian-food "Thali Score" tracker, and a group-chat trivia host (the most speculative). The user's Instagram reel (instagram.com/reel/Dd-QUM2PypU) could not be opened, because Instagram was blocked from the research environment and searches found no mirror or transcript. This report therefore does not respond to whatever that reel specifically claimed.

## Custom GPTs are closing and MCP plugins are the only door left

The naming has changed so many times that sources from different years describe different products. The **2023 "ChatGPT plugins"** (about 1,000 at peak) are long gone. The **January 2024 GPT Store** of custom GPTs came next. Then came **"Apps in ChatGPT"**, built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP), announced on 6 October 2025 with Booking.com, Canva, Coursera, Figma, Expedia, Spotify and Zillow as launch partners ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-apps-in-chatgpt/)). On **9 July 2026 OpenAI renamed apps to "plugins"** and replaced the App Directory with a Plugin Directory. A plugin now bundles skills (instructions, examples, code), apps (MCP connections) and app templates, and existing apps kept working underneath ([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001256-plugins-in-chatgpt-and-codex); [Taskade](https://www.taskade.com/blog/chatgpt-plugins)). So in late-2026 writing, "ChatGPT plugin" means the new MCP-based kind. For a builder, the unit of work is unchanged: a remote MCP server that exposes tools to the model and can return sandboxed HTML/JS widgets. Those widgets render inline, picture-in-picture or fullscreen, and keep their own state through `window.openai.widgetState` ([DeepWiki API reference](https://deepwiki.com/openai/openai-apps-sdk-examples/4.2-window.openai-api-reference)).

DevDay 2026 (29 September) added **plugin extensions**: a sidebar home, persistent interactive panels, custom file viewers, and automations built on the *proposed* MCP Events spec. It also added a Plugin Creator tool and a redesigned submission flow ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-expands-chatgpts-plugins-with-app-like-interfaces-and-automations/); [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/openai-devday-2026/)). These features are three days old as of this writing, and no general-availability dates have been confirmed. A first-week build should not depend on them. MCP Apps also became an official cross-host standard on 26 January 2026, so the same server can run in Claude, which has an interactive-apps directory at claude.ai/directory for paid tiers ([WorkOS](https://workos.com/blog/2026-01-27-mcp-apps); [Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/interactive-tools-in-claude)). Gemini's custom MCP apps are **limited to US users aged 18+**, so they do little for an India-first product ([Gemini Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137?hl=en)).

On 11 September 2026, OpenAI announced that it would **retire custom GPTs in favour of plugins**. The planned steps were a migration banner on 17 September, no new GPTs from 25 September, and GPTs stop running on **11 December 2026**. A GPT's instructions convert into a Skill, but its Custom Actions, conversations and sharing settings do not carry over ([gsmdome](https://www.gsmdome.com/openai-to-retire-custom-gpts-on-december-11-2026-shifting-users-to-plugins-and-skills); [EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/openai-to-retire-custom-gpts-in-december-as-creators-move-to-plugins)). If the brief behind this research included custom GPTs, that half of it is obsolete. This also creates a timing opportunity, though a narrower one than it looks. Prompt-only GPTs in niches like astrology, resumes and fitness can auto-convert into Skills and keep competing. GPTs that depended on Custom Actions (live data, persistence) lose that plumbing and have to be rebuilt as MCP apps. A well-built MCP app has about a ten-week window in which those action-dependent incumbents are disrupted.

## The directory is uncrowded but delivers almost nobody

Fewer competitors does not mean more users. Six months after launch, Bloomberg found apps "hidden away," partners holding back features, a buggy SDK, tedious approval and **no usage data for developers** ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-30/openai-s-chatgpt-app-store-took-aim-at-apple-but-results-lag-so-far)). An Alpic executive told Modern Retail that "people don't even know that there are apps in the ChatGPT store," and that a branded app like Sephora's does not launch from a generic cosmetics question ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/retailers-are-rushing-to-build-ai-apps-its-unclear-if-shoppers-will-use-them/)). DevDay 2026 promised "improved ranking and recommendations," and said that ChatGPT "will soon" recommend relevant plugins mid-conversation ([DEV Community](https://dev.to/techaiwire/chatgpt-plugin-extensions-add-app-panels-and-automations-2fhm)). The word "soon" means proactive suggestion of indie apps should not be counted on yet. A forum report says extra discoverability goes only to "proven apps" ([OpenAI forum](https://community.openai.com/t/chatgpt-apps-metada-for-discoverability/1371136)).

Two discovery mechanics are within an indie's control. First, directory search **weights the app name far more than anything else**, also indexes the short description, and handles single keywords better than long-tail phrases ([WaniWani](https://www.waniwani.ai/blog/chatgpt-app-directory-optimization)). A literal name like "Kundli Match" therefore beats a coined brand. Second, OpenAI's docs say model-driven invocation happens when "tool metadata, descriptions, and past usage align with the user's prompt and memories" ([OpenAI Apps SDK](https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/plan/use-case)). Tool descriptions should be precise "use this when…" contracts. Keyword stuffing can get an app rejected outright ([Moburst](https://www.moburst.com/blog/chatgpt-app-directory-explained-what-brands-need-to-know/)).

The category map shows where an indie cannot win. B2B and SaaS dominate by count: analytics has 70+ apps, CRM 60+, education 60+ and design 45+. Travel, real estate, music and design belong to launch partners ([awesome-chatgpt-apps](https://github.com/rdmgator12/awesome-chatgpt-apps)). In India, **Swiggy already covers food, Instamart and Dineout inside ChatGPT** (cash-on-delivery only) ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/swiggy-enables-grocery-food-delivery-via-chatgpt-and-other-ai-tools-126012701092_1.html)). CarWale, Internshala, College Vidya and LeapScholar are also listed. The July snapshot had **no visible Games or Dating category**, even though gaming and entertainment were among the most popular GPT Store categories ([gptreview.io](https://www.gptreview.io/blog/the-most-popular-gpts-in-the-openai-gpt-store)). Astrology is contested but has no dominant brand: there is one Vedic-chart plugin, Steer Astro ([ChatGPT Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/steer-astro)), plus the standalone KundliGPT. No dominant ChatGPT app turned up for Indian competitive-exam prep.

## Every viral ChatGPT moment so far was a personal verdict

All the documented viral spikes inside ChatGPT came from **first-party features that turned the user's face or data into a recognisable, postable artifact**. Ghibli-style images brought **1 million new users in one hour** ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/04/01/sam-altman-chatgpt-signups-soar-hayao-miyazaki-image-generation-feature)), and **130M+ users generated 700M+ images in the first week**, with India a major growth driver ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/chatgpts-image-generation-explodes-with-700m-creations-in-first-week/)). The action-figure "starter pack" spread through LinkedIn because it printed the user's name and job title on the box ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91318427/the-ai-starter-pack-trend-is-taking-over-linkedin-and-tiktok)). The "roast me based on everything you know about me" prompt ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/everyones-asking-chatgpt-to-roast-them-heres-how-to-try-it)), the Wrapped-style "Your Year with ChatGPT" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/22/chatgpt-launches-a-year-end-review-like-spotify-wrapped/)) and the February 2026 "caricature of me and my job" ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lesliekatz/2026/02/06/chatgpt-trend-turns-people-into-caricatures---and-shows-how-well-ai-knows-us/)) all follow the same formula. The formula is: *about me* → familiar template → copyable one-line trigger → a result posted with an implied "do yours."

Standalone AI hits follow the same pattern and add monetization. **Cal AI** reached **15M+ downloads and $30M+ annual revenue** from a single "photo of food → calorie number" interaction, first spread by fitness influencers and later by paid ads, before MyFitnessPal acquired it ([Yahoo Finance/TechCrunch](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/myfitnesspal-acquired-cal-ai-viral-140000003.html); [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/cal-ai-breakdown)). Remini's AI headshots reached #1 on the US App Store with **1.4B+ TikTok views** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/20/remini-tops-the-app-store-for-its-viral-ai-headshots-but-its-body-edits-go-too-far-some-say/)). The study app Gizmo reportedly ran **30+ faceless TikTok accounts to 70M views and $59K MRR** ([Social Growth Engineers](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/ai-study-app-running-30-faceless-accounts-to-70m-views-and-59k-mrr), secondary). That faceless-network playbook transfers best to a ChatGPT app: screen-record the one-line prompt, show the widget appearing inside ChatGPT, and end on the exact app name to type.

The failures carry equally clear lessons. OpenAI's own **Sora** app passed 1M downloads in under five days. Downloads then fell from a November 2025 peak of about 3.33M, and the app shut down in April 2026 ([ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/sora-app-decline-openai/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/openais-sora-was-the-creepiest-app-on-your-phone-now-its-shutting-down/)). Novelty brings people in, but it does not bring them back, and expensive compute burns cash at viral scale. Backlash is the other recurring failure: privacy warnings around the caricature trend ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kateoflahertyuk/2026/02/09/the-new-chatgpt-caricature-trend-comes-with-a-privacy-warning/)) and mental-health criticism of face-rating apps like Umax ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/07/01/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-faces-mental-health/)). Taken together, a viral ChatGPT app needs four things. The verdict must be fast, personal and comparable. The model must not be able to produce it equally well natively, which requires real data, deterministic computation or persistence. There must be a recurring reason to come back. And generation should run on ChatGPT's own model, not on the developer's GPU bill.

## OpenAI's rules forbid selling anything digital inside ChatGPT

OpenAI's guidelines say apps "may conduct commerce only for physical goods; selling digital products or services, including subscriptions, digital content, tokens, or credits, is not allowed, whether offered directly or indirectly (for example, through freemium upsells)" ([OpenAI App submission guidelines](https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/app-submission-guidelines)). Apps also "must not serve advertisements," must suit **users aged 13–17**, and must not target children under 13 (same source). External checkout on the developer's own domain is the recommended path ([OpenAI Apps SDK: Monetization](https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/build/monetization)). WorkOS reads the post-DevDay rules this way: plugins may sell physical goods and **"may let existing paid customers use entitlements they already own,"** and no revenue share or paid-plugin model exists ([WorkOS](https://workos.com/blog/ai-era-app-store-moment-openai-plugins)). The prohibited categories include gambling, which rules out anything resembling real-money fantasy cricket.

That leaves four legal revenue paths for an indie. The first is **physical goods via link-out**, such as printed reports, merchandise or framed cards. Walmart's data supports link-out: in-ChatGPT checkout converted about 3× worse than a click-through to Walmart.com ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agentic-commerce-discover-in-ai-buy-on-site-2026)). The second is **"sell on the web, honour in ChatGPT"**: the user pays on your site (via UPI/Razorpay, outside ChatGPT) and logs in through OAuth to unlock what they already own. The rule that matters is that the widget must never show an "upgrade" button. The notes could not confirm exactly where OpenAI draws the "indirect" line, so check the live guidelines before relying on this path. The third is **B2B licensing**, for example white-labelling to coaching institutes or college placement cells, negotiated entirely off-platform. The fourth is **audience and brand building** in advance of digital-goods support, which OpenAI says it is "exploring" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/developers-can-now-submit-apps-to-chatgpt/)). "Sponsored quiz packs" are not on this list because they risk breaking the no-ads rule.

India's economics push the same way. OpenAI made ChatGPT Go **free for a year for all Indian users** from 27 October 2025 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/27/openai-offers-free-chatgpt-go-for-one-year-to-all-users-in-india)) and began **showing ads on Free and Go in India in August 2026**, calling India a market where usage is high but payments lag ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/openai-to-start-showing-ads-on-chatgpts-free-and-go-tiers-in-india/)). Apps launched for logged-in users on Free, Go, Plus and Pro outside the EU ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-apps-in-chatgpt/)). The post-rename help article lists web, desktop, Work and Codex, so check that your plugin works for a Free or Go user on Android before launch. The new "Sign in with ChatGPT", which lets Plus and Pro allowances pay for inference in partner apps, mostly helps standalone web products, and it currently has only 16 launch partners ([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/sign-in-with-chatgpt/)). It does little for a mostly Free/Go Indian audience. Hosting is cheap. Vercel, Cloudflare Workers and Supabase all have first-class ChatGPT-app templates that scale to zero ([Vercel](https://vercel.com/templates/next.js/chatgpt-app-with-next-js); [Cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/demos/chatgpt-app/)). The real cost is any inference the server runs itself.

## Ranked shortlist: five apps worth a first week

I ranked the ideas on five things: the strength of the built-in share loop, fit with India's 100M weekly users, whether there is a legal revenue path under current rules, whether a widget does something plain chat cannot, and whether one person can build it in a week. All five keep the developer's own server cheap by letting ChatGPT's model do the language and vision work. The MCP tools handle only deterministic computation, data and state.

| Rank | App | Share loop | Reason to return | Legal money path | Biggest risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kundli Match** (Guna Milan card) | Two-person by design; "check yours with your partner" | New pairings, wedding season, daily panchang | Printed matching booklet (physical); web premium report honoured via login | Ephemeris licensing; matchmaking backlash |
| 2 | **PYQ Arena** (JEE/NEET/UPSC) | Daily score and streak cards in aspirant communities | Daily practice, spaced-repetition mistake book | B2B white-label to coaching institutes; printed mistake-books | Question-bank rights and accuracy |

| 5 | **Group Trivia Host** (Bollywood/IPL/office) | Group invites in 20-person chats | Weekly quiz nights | Off-platform corporate packs only | Group-chat widget support unconfirmed |

### 1. Kundli Match: a two-person compatibility card built for WhatsApp

**Why it could spread:** Each use of this app brings in a second person. A 36-point Ashtakoot (Guna Milan) score is deterministic and comparable, which matches the "what did you get?" mechanic behind roast, Wrapped and caricature trends. It is also a native Indian ritual that families already discuss. Prompt-template sites publishing long "ChatGPT prompt for Kundli analysis" guides show that people already do this by hand ([promptjio](https://promptjio.com/astrology/chatgpt-prompt-for-kundli-analysis)). The widget adds something real: ChatGPT cannot reliably compute nakshatra positions without an ephemeris, so the tool supplies accuracy and the model supplies the warm Hindi or English explanation. A playful "friendship match" and "celebrity match" mode widens the audience beyond marriage and lowers the stakes. Steer Astro covers individual charts, and a shareable two-person card sets this app apart from it.

**How it makes money under current rules:** Sell a **printed, couriered matching booklet** or a framed couple card through external checkout, which counts as physical goods. Also offer a detailed web report bought on your own site, which existing buyers unlock inside ChatGPT by logging in. Keep every purchase prompt off the widget.

**Risks:** The Swiss Ephemeris, the usual astronomy library, is dual-licensed (AGPL or commercial), so check the licence before shipping. Birth date, time and place are sensitive personal data under Indian data-protection law, so store as little as possible. Matchmaking can drift into caste or dowry territory, which invites backlash, so keep the framing light. Steer Astro could add matching. A reviewer could also read a print upsell as an "indirect" digital sale, so make sure the free card delivers full standalone value.

**First week:** On day 1, start individual verification of an OpenAI Platform organization. It gates submission, so begin it before writing any code ([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001040-submitting-apps-to-the-chatgpt-app-directory)). The same day, buy the literal domain and fork the Vercel Next.js ChatGPT-app template. On day 2, implement the eight-koota calculation with unit tests against published reference charts. On day 3, expose one MCP tool, `match_kundli(person_a, person_b)`, with birthplace geocoding, plus a tool description written as a contract ("use when the user asks for gun milan or kundli matching; not for daily horoscopes"). On day 4, build the widget: a score dial, a koota breakdown, a Hindi/English toggle, and a 9:16 card layout carrying the app name as a watermark. On day 5, test inline and fullscreen modes on Android and iOS ChatGPT, including whether the card can be downloaded or only screenshotted. Downloading inside the iframe is unconfirmed, and setting widget state during display-mode changes has crashed iOS ([OpenAI forum](https://community.openai.com/t/setwidgetstate-during-requestdisplaymode-transitions-causes-progressive-screen-crash-on-ios/1374994)). On day 6, add a privacy policy, exact CSP domains, and 5 positive plus 3 negative test cases, then submit with no login required in v1 ([sunpeak](https://sunpeak.ai/blogs/submit-chatgpt-app-as-plugin/)). On day 7, connect the same server to Claude, then record ten faceless screen-capture reels across three accounts that end on "type Kundli Match in ChatGPT."

### 2. PYQ Arena: exam practice that ChatGPT cannot do accurately alone

**Why it could spread:** Indian exam aspirants are a huge, tight-knit and very repetitive audience. A daily "10 PYQs, 7/10, 12-day streak" card is the kind of comparable result study communities pass around. The accuracy gap is documented: earlier ChatGPT versions scored **54/100 on UPSC prelims** ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/ai-chatbot-chatgpt-unable-to-clear-upsc-exams-report-1.1677933491223)). A verified question bank with server-side scoring adds value the model cannot fake. Tutoring and teaching also sit at the top of "practical guidance," which together with information-seeking and writing makes up about 80% of ChatGPT use ([OpenAI/NBER](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf)). It has the strongest retention hook on this list.

**How it makes money under current rules:** License a branded or white-label version to coaching institutes off-platform, and sell printed revision or mistake-books through external checkout. A paid web tier can be honoured inside ChatGPT for users who already bought it, as long as it is never pitched in the widget.

**Risks:** Sourcing the question bank and the rights to it is the main cost. Wrong answer keys would ruin trust. PhysicsWallah, Unacademy and Testbook did not appear in the directory snapshot, but that is unconfirmed, and any of them could enter. Cross-session streaks need a stable user identity, which means OAuth. OAuth adds a demo-account requirement in review, a common rejection cause ([Alpic](https://alpic.ai/blog/why-your-chatgpt-app-is-getting-rejected-and-what-you-can-do-about-it)). So v1 should keep state within the conversation through widget state.

**First week:** On days 1–2, pick one exam and subject (for example NEET Biology, last ten years) and hand-verify about 500 questions into JSON. On day 3, build the MCP tools `get_questions`, `submit_answer` and `mistake_book`. On day 4, build the widget: a timed MCQ card, an explanation reveal and a topic heatmap. On day 5, add the shareable daily-score card and the Hindi toggle. On day 6, write test cases and submit. On day 7, seed the app in aspirant Instagram and Telegram communities with Gizmo-style faceless study reels.

### 3. Resume Roast & Redline: a professional-identity hook with a real UI advantage

**Why it could spread:** The roast prompt and the job-title starter pack both went viral on professional identity, and LinkedIn is where Indian graduates already post. Two-thirds of ChatGPT writing requests edit existing text rather than create new text ([OpenAI/NBER](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf)). A side-by-side diff with per-edit accept or reject buttons and a job-description match score is a clear case of a widget beating plain chat. The shareable piece is a funny "roast score" card, and the reason to return is each new job application.

**How it makes money under current rules:** A web resume builder or job-application tracker, paid off-platform and unlocked by login. College placement-cell (TPO) licensing is a second B2B route.

**Risks:** This has the highest native-feature risk on the list, because ChatGPT already edits resumes well and resume GPTs will convert into plugins. The diff UX and scoring have to be the moat. Resumes contain personal data. Copycats are cheap. Stay away from job listings, where Internshala is already present.

**First week:** On day 1, fork the template. On day 2, build an `analyze_resume(resume_text, jd_text)` tool that returns a structured edit list and a score. On day 3, build the diff widget. On day 4, build the roast card in 1:1 format for LinkedIn. On day 5, run cross-device tests. On day 6, submit. On day 7, post a build-in-public thread on X and LinkedIn and do a Product Hunt launch, which still reports 2M+ monthly users ([LaunchList](https://getlaunchlist.com/blog/how-to-launch-on-product-hunt-2026)).

### 4. Thali Score: Cal AI's loop adapted to Indian food

**Why it could spread:** "Photo of plate → instant number" is the most proven single interaction in consumer AI (Cal AI). An Indian dish database (dal, roti, sabzi, regional thalis) with portion logic gives this app an edge over generic trackers. People already pay for workout and meal prompt packs plus Notion trackers on Gumroad ([Gumroad example](https://progresspro.gumroad.com/l/meal)). Tom's Guide found that ChatGPT-as-coach leaves users to "build your own" tracking ([Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-asked-chatgpt-to-become-my-exercise-coach-heres-what-happened)). The plan is for ChatGPT's own vision to identify the items and for the tool to supply nutrition values, a daily log and a weekly protein report card.

**How it makes money under current rules:** A paid web tracker or app, and grocery or meal-plan link-outs for physical goods. Swiggy's Instamart is already in ChatGPT, so whether an affiliate cut exists is an open question.

**Risks:** Health-accuracy claims, the risk that native vision makes the app unnecessary, and persistence that needs OAuth. This app also demands far more daily commitment than the others, so expect the Sora-style fade unless the logging is very low-effort.

**First week:** On days 1–2, build a 300-dish nutrition table. On day 3, build a `log_meal(items, portions)` tool. On day 4, build the widget with a daily ring and the shareable card. On days 5–6, test and submit. On day 7, post reels of the plate photo turning into a verdict.

### 5. Group Trivia Host: the highest ceiling, gated by one unknown

**Why it could spread:** ChatGPT group chats hold **up to 20 people on every consumer tier** ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/chatgpt-group-chats-are-here-but-not-for-everyone-yet)), yet no app owns group play. Games and entertainment showed real demand in the GPT Store, and 11% of messages are "expressing," which includes play ([OpenAI/NBER](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf)). Bollywood, IPL and office-trivia rounds with a live scoreboard spread through invites by design.

**How it makes money under current rules:** Almost nothing in-chat, because ads and digital goods are banned and sponsored packs risk the ad rule. The realistic route is corporate team-building packs sold off-platform.

**Risks:** The research could not confirm whether Apps SDK widgets render in group chats, or whether several participants can share widget state. That is a blocker. IPL and team marks raise trademark and licensing issues, and anything that looks like fantasy betting runs into the gambling ban.

**First week:** On days 1–2, build a developer-mode spike that tests whether a widget renders and accepts input from multiple people in a group chat. If it does not, pivot on day 3 to a single-player daily puzzle with a shareable streak. If it does, build the host tool and scoreboard on days 3–5 and submit on day 6.

## Conclusion

The question this research started from, "what plugin can go viral inside ChatGPT," gets the platform the wrong way round. Until OpenAI ships the "soon" in-conversation recommendations, ChatGPT is a place where virality happens to the user, not a channel that sends users to developers. So the winning design starts with the share artifact, built for Instagram and WhatsApp, and treats the ChatGPT app as the place where that artifact gets made. An Indian indie has a real edge here. India is OpenAI's second market and a test bed for ads and free tiers, the global SaaS incumbents crowding the directory do not serve its culturally specific rituals and exams, and computations like Guna Milan or verified PYQ scoring are things the model genuinely cannot do on its own.

Two timing bets matter more than polish. The first is the **11 December custom-GPT shutdown**, which disrupts action-dependent incumbents in astrology, resumes and fitness for roughly ten weeks. The second is OpenAI's repeated statement that it is "exploring" digital-goods monetization. A developer who builds an audience and a login-linked web entitlement now will be ready to switch on in-chat revenue the day the rules change. Both should be weighed against the platform's record: OpenAI killed Instant Checkout within six months and shut Sora within seven. Build on the portable MCP Apps standard, keep the business off-platform, and treat any single OpenAI surface as rented land.
