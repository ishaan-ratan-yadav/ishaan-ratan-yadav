# Hashtags (updated 2026-10-04)

## Rules
- **1–2 hashtags on every original post.** Posts with 1–2 relevant tags get more engagement than posts with none or with 5 or more. Three or more reads as spam.
- Put them **at the end** of the post, or work one naturally into the last line. Never in the hook.
- **Formula: 1 community tag + 1 topic or trend tag.** The community tag reaches people who browse that hashtag feed. The topic or trend tag catches today's search traffic.
- If a relevant hashtag is **trending today** (Explore → Trending), use it as the second tag. This is the biggest hashtag win, but only when the post genuinely belongs to that trend.
- **No hashtags in replies**: they look like spam under someone else's post. Quote posts get 0–1.
- Never use an unrelated trending tag to hijack reach (X treats that as spam).
- The weekly review tracks which tags appear on the best posts and reorders this list.
