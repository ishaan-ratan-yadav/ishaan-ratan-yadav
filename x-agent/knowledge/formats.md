# Formats that work in our arena (starter list; the playbook overrides this with real data)

Arena = whole creative space + whole freelancing space, plus viral bridges from anywhere. 3D formats are our proof and edge.

Each format lists the signal it mainly targets. Tag every post with its format name.

## Media formats (our edge: we can make 3D)
| Format | What | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Breakdown** | wireframe → clay → lookdev → final, 8–20 s, text overlay of hours/steps | dwell, bookmarks, follows |
| **Spec ad (trend-jack)** | unsolicited 3D ad for a brand/product that's trending today (launch, meme, news). Speed > perfection: a 5–8 s shot within 24–48 h | reposts, quotes, out-of-network reach |
| **FOOH** | fake out-of-home: CGI object in a real street/landmark shot. Viral category in 2025–26 | shares, "is this real?" replies |
| **Before/after** | client brief vs delivery; v1 vs v14; reference vs render | replies, quotes |
| **Recreate the viral** | "recreated [viral video/meme/ad] in 3D in N hours" | reposts, follows |
| **Satisfying loop** | 3–6 s seamless loop (soft-body, cloth, liquid, product) | dwell, rewatch |
| **AI vs 3D** | same brief done with AI video and with 3D, side by side, honest verdict | replies (debate) |
| **Card** | `make_card.py`: statement / list / versus for takes and lessons | bookmarks, reposts |
| **Timelapse** | sped-up screen record of a build | dwell |

## Viral bridges: connecting "anything viral" to our arena (the core game)
Sources are NOT limited to the niche: news, sports, memes, launches, celebrity moments, internet drama, viral videos,
big brand moves, movie/game trailers. The niche-specific part is the *angle*, not the trend.
Examples of the move: a viral sports moment → "what freelancers can learn from how X handled pressure";
a brand's viral rebrand → ad critique + how we'd have animated it; a meme template → filled with a client/freelance truth;
a viral video → "how this was made" or "recreated in 3D".
For any trending topic, test each bridge and pick the strongest, most natural fit:
1. **Make it in 3D** (spec ad / FOOH / recreate) → if we can produce a visual within the trend's lifetime.
2. **Creative-business lens**: what this teaches freelancers/studios about clients, pricing, attention, branding.
3. **Ad critique**: how a brand should (or did) turn this moment into an ad; storyboard it in text or a card.
4. **Behind-the-scenes lens**: how the viral visual/VFX/ad was made.
5. **Meme format reuse**: the trending meme template, filled with a creative/freelance truth.
Skip if the bridge feels forced, or if the trend is a tragedy, politics or culture war (rule 5).

## Engagement formats (replies & quotes)
- **Value reply**: add a specific fact, number, or experience the original post lacks.
- **Counterpoint reply**: respectful disagreement with a reason. Drives replies to *our* reply.
- **Funny reply**: one-liner that fits the thread. High variance; use on big casual posts.
- **Visual reply**: reply with a relevant render or card (Ishaan attaches it). Very high stop-rate.
- **Quote breakdown**: quote a viral CGI/ad post with "how this was made" + promise a breakdown.
