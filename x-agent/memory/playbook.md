# Playbook (v0.0: research-based starting rules, all UNPROVEN until our own data confirms)

The weekly review promotes a rule to PROVEN only with ≥3 supporting posts from this account. Each rule cites evidence.

## Posting
- [unproven] 3 main posts/day to start, spaced ≥3 h (author-diversity decay). See `schedule.md`.
- [unproven] About half of posts are viral bridges; the rest freelance/business, craft/culture, and proof.
- [algorithm] No links in main posts; link in self-reply. 0–1 hashtag. ≤280 weighted chars.
- [unproven] Media posts (renders, breakdowns, cards) beat text-only on reach for a creative account.

## Hooks
- [unproven] Specific numbers and time/effort stakes in line 1 outperform generic claims.

## Engagement
- [algorithm] Reply to every comment in the first hour after posting.
- [unproven] 10–20 value replies/day under bigger arena accounts drive more follows than our own posts in month 1.
- [Day 1 evidence] At 1 follower, our own posts get 1-24 views. Replies are the ONLY distribution until ~100 followers. Pack order: replies first.

## Media styles
- [unproven, 2026-10-04] Process/wireframe breakdowns and 'AI vs 3D' comparisons are this month's winning creative formats (iProee 2.18M, andrewpprice AI-vs-3D tests).
