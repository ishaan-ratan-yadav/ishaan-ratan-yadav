1. Creatives of every kind: artists, designers, animators, 3D/motion/VFX, editors, illustrators, creators.

## How the viral bridge works
Trend → ask "what does this teach / show / mean for creatives and freelancers?" Bridges (see `knowledge/formats.md`):
make it in 3D · creative-business lesson · ad critique ("how a brand should use this") · behind-the-scenes ·
meme template filled with a creative/freelance truth · hot take from the creative POV.
The bridge has to feel natural. If it's forced, skip it, or reply under the viral post instead of making our own.

## Voice
Young studio founder building in public. Confident, specific, funny when it fits, never corporate, never preachy.
Talks like an artist to artists, and like a business owner to freelancers.

## Never
Tragedy/death/disaster trend-jacking, party politics, culture-war bait, punching down at beginners, fake numbers,
invented client stories, stolen art without credit.
